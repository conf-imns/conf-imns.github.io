2026 International Conference on Intelligent Multimedia, Networking, and Security

3-4 August, 2026

Atlanta, Georgia, USA

IMNS 2026 is Technically Co-Sponsored by the IEEE and IEEE Communication Society

Registration

How to Register

Registration must be completed, and payment received by July 10, 2026 (authors) or July 20, 2026 (others) via the link below:

https://forms.gle/d9uNpuZnZzwRrJNAA

Registration Fees

  1. Conference Full Reg. Fee (covers up to two conference, workshop, or demo/poster papers):
    • IEEE Member ($750)
    • Non IEEE Member ($800)
  2. Workshop ONLY Reg. Fee (covers only one workshop paper):
    • IEEE Member ($400)
    • Non IEEE Member ($450)
  3. Demo/poster ONLY Reg. Fee (covers only one demo/poster paper):
    • IEEE Member ($400)
    • Non IEEE Member ($450)

Conference registration fees include access to conference sessions and exhibit hall, networking events, coffee breaks, banquet and electronic proceedings.

Notes

Paper Uploads: To submit the final paper and all associated attachments, at least one of the authors must register in advance.

Authors who pay an author registration fee are allowed to upload up to 2 papers.

Method of Payment

Registration can only be confirmed once payment is received. Payment can be made by credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover).

Cancellation Policy

The cancellation will only be made in exceptional circumstances with $80 service fee.

Failure to obtain the appropriate visa to travel does not constitute a refund.

The name on the registration can be changed without penalty.
