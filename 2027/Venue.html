Accommodations & Hotels

Hotel blocks and discounted lodging options near the Stevens Institute of Technology campus (Hoboken / Jersey City / New York metropolitan area) for IMNS 2027 attendees will be announced closer to the conference. Attendees are encouraged to check back for group booking codes and reservation links.

Travel & Airport Information

Stevens Institute of Technology is situated on Castle Point in Hoboken, NJ directly overlooking the Manhattan skyline, offering rapid and convenient access to New York City via the PATH train, NY Waterway ferries, and transit. The campus and New York metropolitan area are served by three major international airports:

  • Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) - Newark, NJ (closest airport to Stevens campus)
  • LaGuardia Airport (LGA) - Queens, NY
  • John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) - Queens, NY

All airports provide convenient ground transportation options, including AirTrain connections, commuter rail, express buses, licensed taxis, and rideshare services.
