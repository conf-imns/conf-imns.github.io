Travel & Airport Information

New York City is served by three major international airports offering extensive domestic and worldwide flights:

  • John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) – Queens, NY
  • LaGuardia Airport (LGA) – Queens, NY
  • Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) – Newark, NJ

All airports provide convenient ground transportation options, including AirTrain connections to subway and commuter rail lines, airport shuttles, licensed taxis, and rideshare services.
