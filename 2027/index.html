2027 International Conference on Intelligent Multimedia, Networking, and Security

August 5-6, 2027  •  New York Area, USA

IMNS 2027 is Co-Sponsored by the IEEE and IEEE Communications Society

International Conference on Intelligent Multimedia, Networking, and Security

The International Conference on Intelligent Multimedia, Networking, and Security is a premier global forum that brings together researchers, academics, and industry experts to exchange knowledge and advancements in multimedia technologies, communication networks, and cybersecurity. This conference highlights cutting-edge research and innovative solutions addressing the challenges in intelligent multimedia processing, secure networking protocols, and data protection techniques. The event fosters interdisciplinary collaboration, offering keynote speeches, technical sessions, workshops, and panel discussions to promote the latest trends and practical implementations in the field. The IMNS 2027 conference will take place in the New York Area between August 5-6, 2027, focusing on advancements in multimedia technologies amid competitive academia and industry.
